Building the lakes…

Hot Brook Lakes 3-D

Upper and Lower Hot Brook Lake and the brook between them, at 1/10 of their real size with their real depths (the 1949 survey soundings). The land is drawn at the lakes' scale; the water's depth, and everything that lives in it, is true size. The water and its fish are simulated live: about 17,000 fish of eight species at the real lakes' numbers, each fish (or each school of perch and shiners) with its own brain.
