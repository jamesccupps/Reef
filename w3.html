Hot Brook Lakes 3-D

Upper and Lower Hot Brook Lake and the brook between them, at 1/10 of their real size with their real depths (the 1949 survey soundings). The land is drawn at the lakes' scale; the water's depth, and everything that lives in it, is true size. The water and its fish are simulated live: about 13,000 fish of the eleven species recorded in these lakes, at the real lakes' numbers, each fish (or each school of perch, white perch and killifish) with its own brain.

1× is real time: a day takes a day, and every fish lives it second by second — its brain deciding five times a second, swimming at its real speed. Fast-forward runs the same lives in blocks (each fish's brain decides once a block and it goes where it chose), so a season passes in minutes and evolution can work over years. The water — temperature, ice, currents, O₂, algae, plankton — is simulated live.
