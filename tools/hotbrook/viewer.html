Sources and what is approximate
    • Lake beds: interpolated from the 1949 MDIFW soundings (146 spot depths), with the scanned survey maps fitted onto today's shoreline (median misfit 18 m). The beds may have filled a little with sediment since 1949.
    • Trees are placed by land-cover class at an illustrative density, not counted.
    • Land cover is mapped at 30 m, so narrow shore bands (beach, alder fringe) are wider or missing here. The aerial photo is a summer image.
    • Field notes are first-hand observations from an angler who fishes these lakes.
