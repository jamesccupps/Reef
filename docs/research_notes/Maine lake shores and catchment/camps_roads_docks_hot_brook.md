# Camps, docks, roads and the launch at Upper and Lower Hot Brook Lake

Compiled 2026-10-23. This note covers the human-built features around both lakes and the connecting brook: what was found, how it was checked against aerial photos from many years, and what is still uncertain.

- **Data:** `tools/hotbrook/data/camps_roads.json` (git-ignored). It holds every structure with its lon/lat, footprint, distance to the 2017 lidar waterline, docks, the years it is seen and not seen, the Google frames that cover it, a confidence and notes. It also holds the roads, the launch, the beaches and the lawns.

### Upper Hot Brook Lake (33 dwellings)

**South shore: 18 dwellings (U01–U18).** These sit on Hot Brook Lake Rd, Lydic Ln and Huff Rd, on small Danforth lots 016-004 to 016-028.
- Most are 6–30 m from the water.
- U12 (12 Lydic Ln) and U18 (15/18 Huff Rd) are back lots.
- U09 is low-confidence.
- U11 was seen up to 2021 but **not in 2023 or fall 2024**: it was removed, or the canopy now hides it.

**East cove: 4 (U20–U23).** All are on one 99-ha lot, 006-029, reached by its own road from Rt 169.
- U20 is a large green-roofed building at a gravel lot with 8–12 vehicles in several years, with a dock.
- U21 is a camp and U22 a house or garage built 2009–2011.
- U23 is two trailers placed between spring 2020 and Aug 2021.

**North-east row: 11 (U30–U42).** These are on N Haney Rd, lots 006-042 to 006-047 and 011-063 to 011-065, along the outlet basin and ending at the NE sand beach.
- U35 is the largest building on either lake (about 21×20 m).
- U36 was built 2018–2021 and U42 2015–2018.
- The west and north-west shores (timberland, WA026 01-1) have **no buildings**.

### Launch

**Corrected by the human (2026-10-23), with two Google Earth 2024 screenshots:** "huff road leads to camps". The launch is the **gravel landing at the head of Upper's east cove** (about lon −67.92871, lat 45.60935): the gravel lot that runs to the shore beside U20/U21, reached by the east-cove road's switchbacks. Its 8–12 parked vehicles (2003–05, 2015, 2023) and the boats and canoes on the lawn, read below as U20's, are the launch's. The human: "i think this is it". The green-roofed U20's purpose is unknown to the human too. Rt 169 paved and the camp and town roads gravel: confirmed. Docks: "most camps on the lake or with a path to the lake have a removable dock, a lot are smaller".

*What the review first said (superseded):* an unimproved sand-and-gravel ramp at the end of Huff Rd, at about lon −67.92940, lat 45.60388. Huff Rd is a town road (E911 class "Local") that runs about 240 m from Rt 169 to Upper's south shore.
- The "ramp" is a vehicle-worn strip into the water in every year from 2003–05 to 2024, with a small turnaround: a camp lot's lawn and path.
- In some years there is a narrow dock or boats beside it (low confidence).
- Confidence is medium.
- MDIFW says only "unimproved boat launch site at the south end … accessible from Rt. 169".
- A Danforth land listing places "Hot Brook Lake and its public boat launch" across Rt 169 from Hot Brook Lake Rd. That fits Huff Rd and Hot Brook Lake Rd, which leave Rt 169 about 90 m apart.
- The Maine GeoLibrary boat-launch layer does not list it; that layer covers only improved state and town sites.

## Disagreements and how they were resolved

**MDIFW (2004) said Lower has "no camps"; there are now about 22 dwellings.**
- 55 Lakeview Rd is already in the 2003–05 photo, and 5 and 71 Lakeview are in by 2009. So even in 2004 the statement was not quite right, unless it meant the main shoreline.
- Black Point's camps date from about 2015 on.
- The Woodland Ridge subdivision dates from 2021–2024.
- I trusted the imagery.

**MDIFW mentions camps only on Upper's south shore.** The imagery also shows the NE row (11) and the east cove (4).

**The E911 database is incomplete and partly ahead of the buildings.**
- Upper's NE row of 11 dwellings has only one E911 point, 3 N Haney Rd. That house was itself new between spring 2020 and Aug 2021; its point was created in 2024. OSM's tracing covers this row instead.
- Five Woodland Ridge points (507, 511, 515, 615, 623) mark lots with no building visible.

**MS footprints miss camps under canopy and include fragments.** About 15 dwellings have no footprint; these were placed by eye, with their size given in the notes. Fragments under 15 m² were ignored.

**One OSM building is mis-tagged.** Way 656519726 (camp I11) carries `leisure=stadium`, which is an error.

**Docks: the human says most camps have docks; the imagery shows fewer.**
- About half of Upper's dwellings and almost none on Lower show a dock in any summer image.
- Spring 2020 shows **no docks anywhere**, which confirms they are pulled for winter.
- Fall 2024 still shows 8 of Upper's 17.
- Small docks under overhanging trees, or ones pulled before the 22 Sep 2023 flight, are likely missed, and the 2011–2015 photos are only 1 m.
- I recorded only docks seen in at least one image (see the questions below).

**Where is the launch?** Neither database marks it. I placed it at the only public-road access to the south shore, which matches both written sources.
